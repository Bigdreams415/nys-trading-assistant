## Lessons
- Tight stops lose even when the idea is right. Spikes at the London and NY opens hunt them.
- Buying after a big move (breakouts, late retests) has been the main way Claude's calls lost.
  The user's complaint: "you wait until it has moved, then say buy".
- The trades that worked were at a clear level with room before the next level (range edges),
  including the user's gold buy at support on 21 Oct.
- Don't hold trades overnight unless the SL is at entry.
